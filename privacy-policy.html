Siply Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Siply ("we", "the app") helps you track daily hydration on iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what we collect and why.

Information we collect

  • Profile and onboarding answers: display name, body metrics, goals, habits, sleep window, and similar settings you enter in the app.
  • Hydration data: drinks and foods you log, daily goals, and streaks. This is stored on your device. If you sign in, drink and food logs may also be backed up to your account in Firebase.
  • Onboarding responses (optional cloud copy): during setup, a copy of your onboarding answers may be stored in Firebase and linked to an anonymous install identifier on your device. This helps us improve onboarding. You can use the app without creating an account.
  • Account data (optional): if you create an account or sign in: email address, Firebase user ID, friend code, and friends you add for Ranks. You can sign in with email, Sign in with Apple, or Google. Apple or Google may share your name and email with us. If you use Apple's Hide My Email, we receive a private relay address instead of your personal email.
  • Push tokens (if signed in): a device push token so we can send friend-request notifications.
  • Support messages: if you email us for help.

How we use information

  • Provide hydration tracking, reminders, streaks, home-screen widget data, and optional account features for Ranks.
  • Store account, friend, and optional backup data in Firebase when you are signed in.
  • Send email verification codes and password-reset emails when you create, verify, or recover an account.
  • Send friend-request notifications if you are signed in and notifications are allowed.
  • Respond to support requests.

Where data is stored

  • On your device: hydration logs, goals, streaks, and settings stay in local storage on your iPhone or iPad. The widget reads a summary from shared on-device storage only.
  • Firebase (Google): optional onboarding responses; and, if you sign in, account data, friend data, push tokens, and optional drink/food-log backup in Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore. See Firebase privacy information.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use service providers (such as Firebase/Google, Apple for Sign in with Apple, and email delivery for verification and password-reset messages) only to operate the app.

If you add friends for Ranks, those friends can see your display name, today's hydration, daily goal, and streak. They cannot see your individual drink or food logs.

Your choices

  • Use the app without an account.
  • Sign out or delete your account in Settings. Deleting your account removes your Firebase login, friend connections, Ranks entries, push tokens, and server-side drink/food backups. Friends will see Deleted account. On-device data is removed when you delete the app.
  • Disable notifications in iOS Settings.
  • Delete the app to remove on-device data.

Children

Siply is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
